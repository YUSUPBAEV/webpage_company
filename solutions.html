Solutions

Solutions for the problems AI hardware teams actually face

Most engagements start from a concrete problem, not a service category. Here is how we combine our disciplines to solve the most common ones.

Problem

"Our model is too slow on the target hardware."

The model meets its accuracy target but misses its latency or throughput budget on the device it has to ship on.

What we do
  • Profile the workload to locate compute, memory and operator hotspots
  • Apply INT8 / INT4 quantization and compression with accuracy tracking
  • Restructure operators to map efficiently onto the accelerator
  • Benchmark before and after on the real hardware
Disciplines
Model OptimizationPerformance EngineeringEdge AI
Problem

"We need to validate an accelerator idea before committing to silicon."

An architecture looks promising on paper, but tape-out is expensive and the risks are not yet quantified.

What we do
  • Build cycle-accurate models to explore design-space trade-offs
  • Implement the architecture in RTL and prototype it on FPGA
  • Run representative workloads on real hardware
  • Deliver evidence for the go / no-go and sizing decisions
Disciplines
Performance EngineeringFPGA AccelerationASIC & RTL
Problem

"Our accelerator's compute units sit idle."

Peak compute looks great on the datasheet, but measured utilization is low and memory bandwidth appears to be the limit.

What we do
  • Analyze compute utilization and memory-bandwidth behavior per layer
  • Rework dataflow, tiling and on-chip buffer allocation
  • Optimize DMA scheduling and memory-system architecture
  • Verify improvements with benchmarks
Disciplines
NPU DesignPerformance Engineering
Problem

"We need custom AI IP for our SoC."

An off-the-shelf NPU does not fit the product's area, power or workload profile, and the team needs a dedicated accelerator block.

What we do
  • Define the accelerator microarchitecture around your target workloads
  • Develop verified Verilog/SystemVerilog IP with standard interfaces
  • Support SoC integration and FPGA-based pre-silicon validation
  • Co-design the software interface with your runtime team
