Our publication policy

Evidence before claims

Hardware performance numbers are easy to inflate and hard to reproduce. We hold ourselves to a simple rule: a number appears on this site only if it was measured on real hardware or a validated model, with a documented methodology.

Some projects are under NDA and cannot be published in full. On request, and where our agreements allow, we are happy to walk through relevant work in more detail.
