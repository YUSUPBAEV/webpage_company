About the company

We help companies turn AI models into efficient, high-performance hardware.

We are an AI hardware engineering company focused on turning demanding AI workloads into efficient hardware implementations. Our expertise spans AI model optimization, NPU architecture, FPGA acceleration, RTL design, ASIC/SoC engineering and edge AI.

Technology companies

How we think

Engineering principles

01

Whole-stack thinking

The fastest fix is often in a different layer than the symptom. We look across model, compiler, architecture and RTL before choosing where to act.

02

Measure, then decide

Profiling and modeling come before architecture, and benchmarks come before conclusions.

03

Build it for real

We deliver synthesizable RTL, working prototypes and deployable models, not just slide decks.

04

Honest numbers

We report what we measured, how we measured it, and what we did not test.

05

Knowledge transfer

We document our work so your team can own, extend and maintain it.
